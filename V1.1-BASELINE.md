Captured on 2026-08-30 before any V1.1 cleanup or optimization.

This report is intentionally non-secret. It records two distinct facts:

- the original repository measurements are from protected `origin/main` at
  `b5ff4e879095f6363c88469a1c822a2cce90c877` (tree
  `53df992a21a3a652f99b4b37e39b412299d3d056`) and the parity-closed accounting
  basis is recorded below at `c84df2438aafa2181c8ca6554d27ea7df75ef4ac`
  (tree `e8e4e802358fee61500f6d0c29652cf890496252`);

  `192.168.1.164`, including the already-running Control Center hotfix.

The effective live source was found to be ahead of protected main. V1.1 work
therefore starts by reconciling that byte-verified source into version control;
the cleanup does not treat unversioned live material as disposable.
